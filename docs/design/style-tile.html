Bryel's Care ERP + CRM: design direction (Gate 0)

A calm, warm-neutral workspace with a restrained botanical green, one dark call-to-action per screen, pastel status chips with dark text, and risk states that are unmistakable by icon, label, colour and border, built from 360 px phones up to the owner's desktop.

Phone: Operator mode

360 × 720 frames. Verbs people use, one sticky primary action within thumb reach, 44 px targets or larger, no horizontal scroll. Risk states appear before any action is offered.

What we adapt from the reference and what we change

The reference is the Dribbble "Customer Journey CRM Dashboard" (docs/source/ui-inspiration.md). It is third-party work, used as a direction, not copied.

We adopt

  1. One page title, cards as the unit of layout, whitespace instead of dividers.
  2. Card radius 16 to 20 px, hairline borders, elevation only on overlays and sticky actions.
  3. The solid dark pill for the active navigation item, and the dark tile for the one strongest action.
  4. The stage-board pattern for the Production board (batches by state) and the Order board.
  5. Avatar rows with count badges for assignee load and approval queues.
  6. Donut and sparkline KPIs with clear legends, each paired with a table that drills down.
  7. Pastel-filled status chips with dark text and a leading icon.

We change

  1. Botanical green primary instead of black; black (--cta) is reserved for the single most important action and the active pill.
  2. Warm off-white surfaces instead of cool grey, plus a dark theme that keeps every chip at 7:1 or better.
  3. The persistent left navigation with collapsible groups replaces the top-bar pill menu; the thin icon rail survives only as the tablet rail.
  4. Operators get fewer, larger targets (44 px minimum, 52 to 56 px in operator mode) and card lists instead of wide tables on phones; owners and supervisors keep dense tables on desktop.
  5. Risk states are unmistakable: icon + label + colour + border, and a striped banner wherever a state blocks the primary action. Never colour alone.
  6. Nothing decorative that costs touch space at 360 px; connectors, floating avatars and ornamental rails are dropped on phones.
  7. Every page carries loading, empty, error, success, permission-denied and offline/sync states, which the reference does not show.
