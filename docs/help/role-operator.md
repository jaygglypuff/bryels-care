You make the batches. The tablet tells you what to do next; follow the green button.

## Your day in 5 steps

1. Open **Home**. Under **Your tasks today** the top card is your next job. Press its green button (**Start** or **Continue**).
2. **Get the materials**: bring every material on the list to the weighing area and confirm each one. Only take the lots on the list.
3. **Weigh the ingredients**: put the container on the scale, type the weight and press **Confirm weight**. If a second person must check, they sign on the same tablet.
4. Do each step on the screen in order (for example line clearance, mixing, filling). Press the step's button when it is done. For packaging, type how many were used and thrown away; print the product labels from the button on that step.
5. At the last step press **Finish**. The batch then waits for QA. At the end of your shift fill in **Today's count** (bottom bar).

## Good to know

- Not sure what something is? Tap **What is this?** next to the word.
- If a weight is off, the screen tells you; do not force it, call your site manager.
- **Scan** (bottom bar) finds any label: a lot, a box, a shelf.
