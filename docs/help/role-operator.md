You make the batches. The tablet tells you what to do next; follow the green button.

## Your day in 5 steps

1. Open **Home**. Under **Your tasks today** the top card is your next job. Press its green button (**Start** or **Continue**).
2. **Get materials**: bring every material on the list to the weighing area and tap **Taken** on each line. Only take the lots on the list.
3. **Weigh**: scan the label on the container (or type its last 4 numbers), put it on the scale, type the weight in the unit the card shows (kg or g) and tap **Save**. If a second person must check, they check it on their own phone, on their list **Waiting for you to check**. You go on with the next ingredient.
4. Do each step on the screen in order (for example the Start check, mixing, filling). Tap **Step done**, then **Done — go to …**. On the packing step tap the one green button to print the piece and box labels in one go.
5. The last step is **Move to the QA waiting area**: answer the questions about what was made and what is left over, then tap **Finish and send to QA**. The batch then waits for QA. At the end of your shift write what happened on the order's **Today** tab. **Today** (bottom bar) shows everything written today.

## Good to know

- Not sure what something is? Tap **What is this?** next to the word.
- If a weight is off, the screen tells you; do not force it, call your site manager.
- **Scan** (bottom bar) finds any label: a lot, a box, a shelf.
