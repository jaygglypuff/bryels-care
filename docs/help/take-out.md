Use this when a few finished pieces leave without a customer order: an online shop order (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, your website), a free sample, a gift, the display shelf or staff use. Write it down when you take them, so the stock stays right.

## Take a few out

1. Open **Take a few out** (Home, or Stock › Take a few out).
2. **Scan the box** you take the pieces from. Or tap the product: the app picks the place for you, the online shop shelf first, then an open box or loose pieces, then the one that expires first.
3. Say **how many**. Use **+** and **−**. For more than one product (today's online orders: 2 lotion, 1 soap), scan or tap the next product; each one gets its own line.
4. Say **why**: Sold online (tap the shop, type the shop's order number if you have it), Sample for a customer (choose the customer or type a name), Giveaway, Display, Staff use, or Other (write what happened).
5. Tap **Take out**. The app saves it once, even if you tap twice or the signal drops and you try again.

## A closed box

Taking pieces from a closed box opens it. The app then counts it as an open box with the pieces that are left.

## What the app will not let out

- Stock QA has not released, stock stopped by QA, expired stock.
- Boxes set aside for a customer order, and anything in the sending-out bay.
- More pieces than are there.

## Waiting for the manager's OK

More than 10 pieces of one product, or more than 30 pieces by one person in a day (the owner sets these numbers), wait for the site manager's or the owner's OK. Nothing leaves the stock until the OK. You see "Waiting for the manager's OK".

## Undo

1. Wrong product or wrong number? Tap **Undo** right after saving, or open the take-out and tap **Undo this take-out**. Say why.
2. You can undo your own take-out the same day. After that, the site manager undoes it.
3. The pieces go back into their box. The take-out stays in the list as **Undone**; nothing is erased.

## Count what's left

1. Open **Count what's left**. Boxes nobody counted or took from for a few days are at the top, marked **Not counted for N days**.
2. Tap the box. Count the pieces in your hand and type the number.
3. **It matches**: one tap. Done.
4. **A difference**: choose why (for example "Taken out but not written down") and add a note. It goes to the site manager as a stock fix. Stock changes only after the OK.

## Reminders

- A box with no take-out and no count for a few days shows in your messages: "Count box …". It goes away when you count it.
- Once a week (Monday unless the owner changes it) you get "Weekly count": count every open box.

## Taken out

The **Taken out** list shows every take-out with the day, the products, the reason or shop and the person, and totals by product, reason, person and day. You can download it for Excel. Money from online shops is kept outside the app for now; the list shows pieces only.

## An online shop shelf

The site manager can mark one shelf as the **Online shop shelf** (Set-up › Shelves and areas). Move a few boxes there once; Take a few out then takes from it first.
