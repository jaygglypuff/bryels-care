Buying goes: what to buy → ask to buy → manager OK → purchase order → send to the supplier → delivery → any delivery problem.

## What to buy

1. Open **Buy & stock → What to buy**. The top list shows what is short now: what production orders still need, and items below the minimum to keep, minus what is free and what is already coming.
2. Press **Ask to buy N** on a row. The request opens filled in: the item, how much, the usual supplier and the last price.
3. Type the price you expect. Without a price, the purchase order needs the manager's OK a second time.

## Make the purchase order

1. On an approved request, press **Make the purchase order**. The supplier the request named is chosen; **change** keeps the request.
2. Step 2 shows **Price per kg** (or per piece) filled in from the request, the last order or the supplier's price. If it differs from last time, it says so. You cannot go on without a price.
3. Step 3: **Arrives on** starts at the day the request needs it, never today or a Sunday.
4. Step 4 says whether the request's OK covers the order. Up to the amount the request was approved for, there is no second OK; above it, the manager approves it again.

## Send to the supplier

1. On an approved order, press **Send to the supplier**. The order is sent in every case. If email is not set up or fails, it says so: print the order and give it to the supplier, or call them.

## Delivery problems

1. When the warehouse writes down a delivery as less than ordered, damaged or a wrong item, you get a message and it shows under **Buy & stock → Buying → Delivery problems**.
2. Choose what was agreed with the supplier: **The supplier sends the rest** (the order stays open), **Ask the supplier for money back** (finance is told to expect it) or **Not needed – close the rest**. Type what was agreed. The message clears.
