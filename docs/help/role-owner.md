You see the whole business. You do not need to do floor work; you decide and you check.

## Your day in 5 steps

1. Open **Home**. Read **Needs your attention**. Tap a line to open what it is about (for example a late customer order).
2. Tap **To approve** (top of the screen, the number shows how many). Press **Approve** on each line, or **Open** to read everything first. As the owner you may also approve your own requests.
3. Look at the four cards on Home: **Sales today**, **Production progress**, **Stock to look at**, **Waiting for your approval**. Tap a card to see the orders, batches or lots behind the number.
4. At the bottom of Home tap **Reports**. You see **Sales, last 4 weeks**, **Batches done this week**, **Stock to look at**, the chart **Sales per week** and **What sells best**.
5. When something looks wrong, open **Full history of changes** (on Home, under **Recent activity**). It shows who changed what, when and why. Nothing there can be deleted.

## Good to know

- **All my sites** (top of Home and Reports) shows every plant together.
- QA decides if a lot or batch is OK to use. You cannot do that for QA; this is the cosmetics rule.
- Changing settings, who says OK to what, and people's jobs is under **Menu → Management**.
