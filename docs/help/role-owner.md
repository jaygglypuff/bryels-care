You see the whole business. You do not need to do floor work; you decide and you check.

## Your day in 5 steps

1. Open **Home**. The green box says what YOU should do next. When nothing needs you it says so, and names who else is working ("Waiting on others: Nina (QA) checks 1 finished batch").
2. Look at the four cards: **Sales this month**, **Customers owe us**, **We owe suppliers** and **Needs you**. Tap a card to see what is behind the number. A zero is grey, never red.
3. Tap **Needs me** (top menu; on a phone, the bottom bar). It has three tabs: **To approve**, **Problems & alerts** and **Messages**. Press **Approve** on each line, or **Open** to read everything first. As the owner you may also approve your own requests, and approve a recipe and its steps alone (no separate QA review; a setting only you can change).
4. The top menu follows your questions: **Sell** (orders, customers, deliveries), **Make** (production, the plan, quality), **Buy & stock** (stock, what to buy, purchase orders, suppliers) and **Money** (invoices, bills, payments, reports). Each has at most five tabs and one **More** list.
5. When something looks wrong, open **Full history of changes** (on Home, under **Recent activity**). It shows who changed what, when and why. Nothing there can be deleted.

## Good to know

- **All my sites** (top of Home and Reports) shows every plant together.
- QA decides if a lot or batch is OK to use. You cannot do that for QA; this is the cosmetics rule.
- Products, recipes, materials, people, settings, labels, shelves, machines and record numbers are in **Set-up**: tap your initials (top right), then **Set-up**. You can also type what you want in **Search** (for example "settings" or "new customer").
- Five choices you asked to keep are in **Settings**. Each starts on the recommended choice, so nothing changes until you change it:
  - **Scan and search → A typed label number counts as a check.** On: a checker may type the number printed on a label. Off: the checks need a scan. Ask QA first; only you can say OK to a change.
  - **Scan and search → Everyone can use the search.** Off: only people who may see stock can search.
  - **How screens look → Big screens: search or Scan button in the top bar.** Search (default): the **Search** box in the middle of the top bar. Scan: the **Scan** button stands there instead; **Ctrl+K** or **/** still opens the search.
  - **How screens look → Fold the change form on setup pages.** Off: the form on a material, product, supplier, site or shelf page is always open.
  - **Sales → Show the invoice step to people who cannot open invoices.** Off: they only see "Delivered".
