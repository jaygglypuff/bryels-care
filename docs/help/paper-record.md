When the internet stops, the work does not stop. You print the batch record, write on it with a pen, and type it in when the internet is back.

## Before: get it ready (needs internet)

1. Open the batch. In **Paper batch record**, tap **Get the paper record ready**. The system makes one page for every step. Each page has its own DOC number and QR code.
2. Wait for **Ready to print without internet ✓**. Now this phone or tablet keeps a copy.
3. Open **My jobs** once a day while the internet works. The paper records of today's and the next batches are saved on the device.

## When there is no internet

1. Open the batch and tap **Print the paper record**. If the batch page does not open, open the **Offline workspace**, tap **Paper records**, then **Print the paper record**.
2. Write with a pen: the lot number, the weight, the time on the clock, your name. The checker writes their name too.
3. Weighing on paper is allowed only when the system cannot be reached. Write why on the page (for example: no internet).
4. If a weight is outside the allowed range, do not hide it. Write what the scale shows.

## After: type it in (needs internet)

1. Open the batch and tap **Enter from the paper record**.
2. Go page by page, in the same order as the paper. For each line type the lot, the weight, **the time written on the paper**, who did it and who checked it.
3. A weight outside the range, or a reading outside the limits, is saved as written. QA gets a problem report and decides.
4. A lot that was on hold or waiting for QA is not saved. QA is told. Keep the page and type that line after QA answers.
5. Give the written pages to your supervisor or QA. They scan them and attach them. Another person, who typed none of it, checks the typing against the scan. The batch cannot pass QA before that.

## If the paper was not used

1. If the system worked and nobody wrote on the pages, a supervisor taps **The paper record was not used** and says why. The blank pages are closed.
2. Pages with lines typed from them are kept.

## Good to know

1. Signing out deletes the saved copies from the device.
2. Print once. If you must print again, write "copy" on the new pages and keep both.
