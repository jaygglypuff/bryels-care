Money in: invoice → in the books → given to the customer → paid. Money out: supplier bill → checked against the order and the delivery → in the books → paid.

## Invoices

1. An invoice draft is made when a delivery is sent out. Check the items. If it says **Tax: not set up yet – ask your accountant**, the invoice has ₱0.00 tax until the accountant sets the rates.
2. Below the amount the owner set (₱50,000 unless changed), press **Save to the books** with your password: one person, one step. At or above it, **Send for approval**: another person approves it first.
3. The invoice now says **In the books – not given to the customer yet**. Press **Email to <their email>**, or **Print the invoice** and then **I printed it and gave it to the customer**. Only then it says **Given to the customer**.

## Payments

1. On an invoice, press **Record a payment**. The amount and the invoice are filled in.
2. The payment shows **Will pay on invoices or bills** and **Left over (kept on their account)**.
3. Below the owner's amount, press **Save to the books** (one step). Above it, it goes for approval.

## Supplier bills

1. **Enter a supplier bill**: choose the supplier (or pick the delivery: the supplier is filled in). Each item is in the order's unit (kg, pieces) with the order's price filled in. If your price differs from the order, it says so while you type.
2. If the check says **Does not match**, choose: **I typed it wrong – change the bill**, **The supplier charged more – enter their money back**, or **Accept the difference (with the reason)**.
3. A draft bill can be changed under **⋯ More → Change the bill**. A bill already in the books with nothing paid is fixed with **⋯ More → Fix a mistake**: it is undone (kept on record) and a copy opens to change.

## Close the month

1. **Close the month** lists what is not finished for each month: deliveries not billed, deliveries without a supplier bill, invoices, bills, payments and credits not finished. Each is a link.
2. A month is closed after its last day. The current month can only be closed with **Close anyway (with the reason)**.
