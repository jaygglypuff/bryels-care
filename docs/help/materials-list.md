The Materials list shows every ingredient of the batch: how much, which lot to use first and where it is. You bring each item to the mixing area and tap **Taken**.

## Take the materials

1. Open the order and tap **Get materials**.
2. The list is grouped by place in the warehouse (Raw materials, Packaging, and so on). Wrappers, labels and boxes are on the list too ("for wrapping, not weighed"): take them now; they are used at the wrap step.
3. Take the lot that is shown. It is the one to use first.
4. Tap **Taken** on the line. The line turns green and shows your name.
5. If you brought everything, tap **All taken** at the bottom.
6. Tapped by mistake? Tap **Taken** again to undo it. You can undo until the list is complete.

## When the list is complete

1. When every line is taken, the screen says **Materials ready**.
2. Tap **Start** to do the Start check.

## A line shows a red warning

1. "On QA hold", "Waiting for QA" or "Expired" means the lot cannot be used. The **Taken** button is off.
2. Ask QA to choose another lot. Never take a lot that is on hold.

## Not needed

1. Under **More details**, tap **Not needed** only when the item is already in the mixing room. Say why.
2. "Not needed" does not skip the weighing. You still weigh the ingredient on the Weigh screen.

## Settings your site may use

- **Scan each lot**: the button says **Scan**. Scan the label on the container; a wrong lot is refused.
- **Move stock to the production area**: each tick also moves the lot in the stock records. Only the storekeeper can tick then.
