You decide if materials and products are OK to use. Nobody else may do this.

## Your day in 5 steps

1. Open **Home** and read **Needs your attention**: batches and lots waiting for you, oldest first.
2. Tap **Check a batch or delivery** (bottom bar). The oldest batch is at the top. Press **Check this batch** (or **Check quality** on a card).
3. Look at how the batch went and its test results. Then press **OK to use**, **Not yet**, or **Not OK – don't use**. Add a note when you say not OK.
4. For deliveries from suppliers, use the same screen, **Check a batch or delivery** (Menu → Operations). Open each lot, check it, and decide **OK to use** or **Not OK**. Many lots at once: **Decide many lots at once** (Menu → Operations → Quality).
5. If something is wrong, stop it: open **Stopped by QA** and press **Stop a lot**; it is put on hold so nobody can use it. When something went wrong in production, open the batch or lot and use **⋯ More → Report a problem**.

## Good to know

- The person who made a batch may not be the one who passes it, unless your company switched that on.
- **Who received it (recall check)** (Menu → Operations → Quality) shows every customer who got a lot, for a recall.
- Your decisions are kept in the history of changes with your name and time.
