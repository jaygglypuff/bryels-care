A recipe says what goes into one batch, how each thing is added, and the steps. It is used only after it is approved.

## Add an ingredient

1. On **Ingredients**, tap **Add an ingredient** and choose it by name.
2. Under **How is it added?** tap one way:
   - **Weigh it**: type the grams (or the percent). This is the usual way.
   - **Count it**: for example **5 sachets** or **2 bottles**. Choose what you count. 0 as the allowed difference means exactly.
   - **Measure it in mL**: for a liquid poured into a jug. The screen works out the grams from the grams in 1 mL.
   - **Add the whole container**: the whole bag, drum or pail goes in. Nothing is weighed.
   - **Add until it is right**: for example citric acid until the **pH is 5.0 to 5.5**. Type your guess and **At most**. More than that needs QA.
   - **My own way**: give it a name (**Scoop with spoon #3**) and a unit (**scoop**). Type how much one scoop is, or tick **Not measured** so the worker writes what was used.
3. The screen shows the grams next to it (**= 125 g**). The stock is always counted in grams (or mL or pieces).
4. Optional: write a **Tip for the person adding it**. It shows on the floor screen and on the paper record.

## Packs and mL

1. To count sachets, the ingredient needs its pack: **1 sachet holds 25 g**. To measure mL it needs **grams in 1 mL**.
2. Add them on the ingredient line (**Add a pack size**) or on the material page under **Packs and mL**.
3. Copy the numbers from the supplier's label or data sheet. QA checks them when the recipe is approved.
4. A size never changes by itself. **Change the size** makes a new version; batches made before keep the old one.

## Bigger or smaller batches

1. **Grow with the batch**: twice the batch, twice the amount.
2. **Same for every batch**: the amount never changes.
3. **Grow with the batch, whole sachets only**: choose **Round up**, **Round down** or **Round to the nearest**. Each batch says "Rounded up from 7.5 to 8 sachets".

## Steps

1. On **Steps**, tap a block: **Heat**, **Mix**, **Homogenise**, **Keep warm for a time**, **Check how it looks**, **Take a QA sample**, **Take a photo**, **Put on labels**, **Close and seal**, **Clean the tools**, **Checklist** and more.
2. **My own step**: give it a name and what to do. Then add what the worker fills in: a number with its unit and lowest/highest OK, yes or no, **choose from a list**, a short note, things to tick, **a photo**.
3. Tick **Has a timer** when the step must wait (for example 30 minutes).
4. Tick **Needs a second person to check**, or **QA must sign** when only QA may sign.

## Save for later

1. **Save this step for later recipes** keeps your own step at this site.
2. **Save this way for later recipes** keeps your own way of adding.
3. Next time, pick it under **Steps saved before** or **Use a way saved before**. The recipe gets a copy.

## Send for approval

1. On **Check and save**, read the summary. Lines that are not weighed say how they are added.
2. Tap **Send for approval**. The same people approve as always. Nothing is used before that.
