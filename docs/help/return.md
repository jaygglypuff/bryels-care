Goods that a customer sends back are scanned against the delivery they left on. A second person approves the return. Then the goods are taken in and kept apart until QA decides what happens to them.

## Start the return

1. Open **Sales & Customers → Quotes, prices & returns → Customer returns** and press **New customer return**.
2. Choose **Which delivery did it come from?**, **Why did it come back?** and **Where to keep it** (the usual returns place). Some reasons need a few words under **Explain**.
3. Press **Start the customer return**.

## Scan what came back

1. Under **Which box or lot came back?**, scan the label of each box or lot. Or tap it in the list of what left on this delivery (that is saved as typed by hand).
2. Type **How many came back**. It cannot be more than was sent on this delivery.
3. Choose **How does it look?** and add a note if needed. Press **Add to the return**.
4. A line added by mistake: press **Remove** on it.

## Approve and take it in

1. When every box is scanned, press **Send for approval**.
2. A different person opens the return and presses **Approve the return**. Or, under **Send back to be fixed**, they write **What needs fixing?** and press **Send back to draft**.
3. When the goods are in the returns area, press **Take it in (kept apart for QA)** and confirm. Each line gets its own return lot, linked to the lot that was sent. The status shows **Back in, waiting for QA check**.
4. **Print return receipt** prints the paper for the customer or the driver.

## QA decides, then finish

1. QA opens each return lot from its line and decides: OK to sell again, stop, or not OK. Nothing is sold or thrown away before that.
2. When QA has decided every lot, press **QA has decided every lot**.
3. Press **Finish the return**. The return is done.
4. To stop a return before it is taken in: **⋯ More → Cancel this return**, say why.
5. Credit notes for returns are not made here yet. Finance makes them separately.
