Returned goods are scanned against the delivery receipt they left on, approved, and received into quarantine. QA decides what happens to them.

## Open the return

1. Open **Customer returns** and press **New return**.
2. Choose the **Shipment (delivery receipt)**, the **Reason** and the quarantine location under **Receive into**.
3. Press **Open return**.

## Add the lines

1. Under **Scan the box or lot label**, scan each returned box or lot.
2. Enter the **Quantity returned**. It cannot be more than was shipped on this delivery receipt.
3. Choose the **Condition**, and add a note if needed. Press **Add line**.

## Approve and receive

1. Press **Submit for approval**. A different person presses **Approve return**, or **Send back to draft** with what needs correcting.
2. When the goods are physically in the quarantine area, press **Receive into quarantine** and confirm. The stock gets a new lot linked to the shipped lot, in quarantine.
3. **In quarantine, waiting for QA**: QA decides (release, rework or reject). Then **Mark dispositioned** and **Close return**.
