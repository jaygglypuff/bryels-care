When every step of the batch is done, you finish it. Finishing counts what was made and puts the batch in the QA waiting area. Then QA checks it. You can only finish once, so count carefully.

## Open Finish

1. Finish is the last step of the batch, **Move to the QA waiting area** (for example "Step 7 of 7"). After the packing step tap **Done — go to Move to the QA waiting area**.
2. It looks like every other step: the round back button, the batch number, one green button at the bottom.
3. Finishing needs a connection. It does not work offline.

## Check what was made

1. If people wrote the good pieces and rejects in the daily log, you see them here. If a number is wrong, tap **Change** and type the right number.
2. If you printed box labels, the good pieces are filled in for you: the pieces in boxes plus the samples.
3. **Samples kept** is filled in for you (usually 2). These come out of the good pieces and are not sold.
4. You see "Expected 123, made 118 (96 %)". If it is outside the OK range, or the rejects are more than allowed, **What happened?** appears. Write what happened (at least 10 characters). A problem report goes to QA.

## Wrappers, bottles and boxes

1. For each one the screen asks one question, already worked out: **All 96 bottles used, none broken?** or **94 used and 2 left over, none broken?**
2. Right? Tap **Yes**.
3. Not right? Tap **No, count them** and type **How many are left over?** and **How many were damaged?** Type 0 if none.
4. An empty count is never taken as 0: Finish says "Count the bottles: how many are left over? Type 0 if none."
5. The line under the question shows the sum, for example "96 taken = 94 used + 0 damaged + 2 left over ✓".
6. Missing ones: count again, or write what happened. Too many: count again; Finish stays off until it is right.
7. After Finish, a card says where the left-over ones go. Tap **Put them back on the shelf**.

## Finish the batch

1. **Goes to** says the QA waiting area. To choose another one, open **More details**.
2. If the recipe asks for a second person to check the counts, there are two ways:
   - **On their own phone** (always there; the only way when your company turned off checks on your phone): tap **Send the counts to be checked**, then **Yes, send**. The screen says "Waiting for Jun or Dino to check the counts". They see **Check the counts** on their list **Waiting for you to check**, look at the real pieces and tap **Looks right**. The batch goes to the QA waiting area by itself. You can go on with other work.
   - **They are here (their password)**: they choose their name and type **their own password** on your phone.
3. With the password: tap **Finish and send to QA**. The screen asks: "You can't change the counts after this." Tap **Yes, finish**, or **Back** to look again.
4. You go back to the order. The green message at the top says the batch is finished and who checks it next. Nothing more for you on this batch.

## The second person said "Not right"

1. You get a message, and Finish shows what they said, for example "Jun said not right: Wrong count".
2. Count again, change the numbers and tap **Send the counts to be checked** again.
3. To change the counts while they still wait, tap **Take the counts back to change them**.
4. You can never check your own counts.

## Something is wrong

- **Finish one step first**: a step of the batch is not done yet. Go back and do it.
- **The counts do not add up**: count again. Missing ones: write what happened. Too many: count again.
- **Pressed twice**: nothing happens twice. The batch is finished only once.
