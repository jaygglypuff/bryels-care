Move a box or a lot from one location to another in three scans.

## Scan box or lot

1. Scan the label on the box or the lot. The card shows what it is, where it is and its QA status.
2. **This box cannot be moved**: the stock is on hold, frozen by a count, or not released. Do not move it; tell your site manager.

## Quantity

1. A whole box moves complete. For stock not in a box, type **How much?** and check the unit (**kg** or **g**, **L** or **mL**, **pcs**). It starts at what is there, for example "60 kg not in a box". You can also type "20 kg".
2. You cannot move more than is there.

## Scan destination

1. Scan the location label where you put the goods.
2. **This stock cannot go there**: the place does not accept this stock (for example stock waiting for QA onto a storage shelf). Choose another place.

## Confirm move

1. Check the summary and press **Confirm move** once.
2. Made a mistake? Use **Undo** within the time shown. After that, ask a site manager to reverse the move.
3. **Already moved** means the same move was just saved; nothing was saved twice.
