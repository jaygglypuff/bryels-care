The Weigh screen shows one card per ingredient, in formula order. The card shows how much to weigh, the OK range and the lot to take.

## Weigh one ingredient

1. The card says **Take lot …0010 · Extracts shelf**. Take that container.
2. Scan the label on the container, or type its last 4 numbers. The card says **Label scanned** or **Lot number typed** with a ✓.
3. Put it on the scale. The card asks in the unit of the scale: **kg** for 1 kg or more (for example **17.919 kg**), **g** for small amounts.
4. Type the weight on the keypad under the field. The card shows **OK**, or "Too much by …" / "Too little by …".
5. Tap **Save** under the keypad. A green line says **Saved: Purified Water 17.919 kg**.
6. The next ingredient opens by itself.

## Second check

1. A card with the chip **Second check** needs a second person.
2. After **Save** you go on with the next ingredient. You do not wait.
3. The checker checks it **on their own phone**: their Home shows **Check**, and their list **Waiting for you to check** shows your weighings of this batch on one card. They tap **Looks right**.
4. If your company allows it, a checker standing next to you can sign on your phone: tap **The checker is here: sign on this phone**, they choose their name and type **their own password**. A name alone is never enough.
5. You can never check your own weighing.

## No signal

1. Tap **Save** with no signal: what you typed stays on the card.
2. If this phone may work offline, the card says **Saved on this phone. It will send when you have signal.** It sends by itself later.
3. If not, the card says **No signal. What you typed is kept here. Tap Save again when you have signal.**

## Wrong lot

1. If the label is for another ingredient, the card says so and which lot to take. Take the right container.
2. Expired lots, stopped lots and lots waiting for QA are never taken.

## Container almost empty

1. Tap **Container almost empty? Use two lots** under the card.
2. Weigh from the first container, then from the next lot. Both are recorded.

## Use another lot (same ingredient)

1. The lot you took is used up, damaged or you can't reach it? Tap **Use another lot**. The other lots of the same ingredient show up. The one to use first is on top.
2. Scan the label of the lot you take.
3. The card says: "This is not the lot you took at Get materials. You can continue — QA will see a note."
4. Tap why: **The first lot is used up**, **The first lot is damaged**, **I can't reach it** or **Other** (write a few words).
5. Weigh and tap **Save**. The lot change and your reason go on the batch record.

## Use another ingredient

1. No coconut oil at all? Look under **No … ? Use another ingredient**.
2. If the recipe allows another one, tap **Use the approved alternative**. It is for this batch only.
3. If not, tap **Ask to use something else**. Choose it by name, check the amount, write why, tap **Ask QA**.
4. The card says **Waiting for QA (Nina) to say yes**. Weigh the other ingredients meanwhile.
5. You can never decide this alone. Only QA says yes.

## Too much or too little

1. Tap **Weigh again** and correct the amount.
2. If you cannot fix it, tap **Ask for approval**: QA or the site manager types their own password and writes the reason; a problem report is opened. Never save a weight you did not read from the scale.

## When every ingredient is weighed

1. The screen shows "4 of 4 weighed" and the total, and how many weighings still wait for the second check.
2. Tap **Step done**. Then tap **Done — go to …** for the next step.

## The scale cannot be used

1. The card says why, for example "SC-01 needs cleaning after the last batch".
2. If it needs cleaning, clean it and press **Record cleaning of SC-01**.
3. If the calibration is overdue, you cannot fix it here. The card says who fixes it.
