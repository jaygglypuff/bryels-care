You run one plant: you plan what is made, you say OK, and you make sure nothing waits.

## Your day in 5 steps

1. Open **Home** and read **Needs your attention**. Late orders, late batches and stock below the minimum come first. Tap a line to open it.
2. Tap **To approve**. Press **Approve** for each request you agree with (production orders, stock corrections, orders to suppliers), or **Open** to read it first.
3. Open **Operations** (bottom bar). **From delivery to dispatch** shows how many things are at each step right now. Red means late or stopped. Tap a step to see the records.
4. To plan making a product, press **New production order** under **Start something** on the Operations Center. Fill in the product and how many, then press **Create production order**. Give it to an operator if you like.
5. Before you go home, open the **Stock to look at** card on Home: buy more of what is running low, and use first what expires soon.

## Good to know

- The batch numbers, lot numbers and order numbers are made by the system. You never type them.
- If a step says it is waiting for someone, it also says who.
