Get each box from the shelf by scanning it. A different person checks the boxes. Stock leaves the books only when the delivery is sent out.

## Get from shelf

1. Open **Sell → More → To get from shelf** (warehouse: on your Home) and press **Get from shelf** on the first order.
2. Go to the shelf the screen shows. Under **Scan the box label, or type the number printed on it**, scan each box. The scan will not work? Type the box number from the label, for example BX-00000005 (or just its last digits, 0005). Boxes made at Finish have their own box label: scan it. You do not need a lot label for them. A box that is not on the list is refused. Scanning the same box twice counts once.
3. Scanned the wrong box? Press **Undo this scan**.
4. Press **Finish getting from shelf**. If a box is missing, tap **A box is missing** and say why.
5. A label will not scan? Tap why (**Label torn**, **Scanner broken** or write another reason), then tap the box in the list. Without a reason the tap is refused.

## Check the boxes (a different person)

1. Open the delivery. The boxes to check are at the top; the next one is marked. Scan each box label, or type the number printed on it. Tapping the list does not count. Loose pieces: scan or type the lot label, then type **How many loose pieces of this lot?**
2. The person who got the boxes from the shelf cannot check the same delivery.
3. A box that is not on the pick list is refused. It is not counted. Put it aside. Only a manager can allow one more box (**Allow it**), with where the customer said so and their password.
4. A box still in the QA cage, or a box for another order, is always refused.
5. If the screen says "548 of 500 pcs are checked, 48 too many", you cannot finish. Open **Boxes on this delivery and scans** and take the extra box off.
6. Fill in **Boxes on the truck**, then press **Finish the check**.

## Send out

1. Open the delivery (**Sell → More → Send out**). First tap **1. Make the delivery receipt for the driver**, then **1. Print the delivery receipt**: the driver needs this paper before the truck leaves. It does not take stock out.
2. **Driver** and **Plate number** are filled in from the last truck that left. Change them if another driver goes. Then press **The truck is leaving: send out now**. This takes the stock out and numbers the delivery receipt.
3. Print the delivery receipt for the driver's copies.

## Proof of delivery

1. When the signed copy comes back, open the delivery and press **Save proof of delivery**.
   - If Save says something is wrong (for example the time is before the truck left), fix it and press **Save proof of delivery** again. What you typed and the signature stay; you do not need to sign again.
2. **Send out** lists deliveries still waiting for their proof of delivery.
