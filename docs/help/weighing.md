Record each ingredient you weigh for the batch. The system checks the lot and the tolerance. A second person checks the weighing.

## Before you weigh

1. The weighing-area line clearance must be signed first (see "Line clearance").
2. Use a scale that shows as available. A scale that is overdue for calibration or is out of service is blocked.

## Scan the lot

1. Under **1. Scan the lot label on the container**, scan the container you are weighing from.
2. The wrong material is always refused. A lot on hold, in quarantine or expired is refused too.
3. If the lot is in several places, choose the **Location**.

## Weigh

1. Under **2. Scale**, choose the scale you used.
2. Under **3. Net weight**, enter the net weight. The target and the allowed range are shown. You can also enter **Gross** and **Tare**, and the net is calculated.
3. Press **Confirm weight**.

## Outside tolerance

1. If the weight is outside the range, the button reads **Request exception**. Weigh again if you can.
2. An exception needs an approver from QA and opens a deviation. Never post a weight you did not read from the scale.

## Check and corrections

1. The checker opens the weighing and presses **Check this weighing**. The checker must be a different person from the one who weighed.
2. A mistake is corrected with a reason. The original stays visible, struck through.
